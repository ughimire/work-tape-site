Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Work Tape is a calculator and estimating app for iPhone and iPad. It is designed so that your data never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Work Tape has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet.

Where your data lives

Projects, calculations, estimates, your business profile, logo and price book are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. If you use the optional Backup feature, the backup file is created on your device and you choose where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, email, etc.). Quotes you share as PDF go through the system share sheet to the destination you pick.

Permissions

Work Tape does not request location, contacts, camera or photo-library access. Choosing a logo uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the single image you select.

Children

Work Tape is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
